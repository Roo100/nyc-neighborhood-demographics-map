Five NYC Charter ballot proposals

  1. Public space permissions: Change reviews for sidewalk cafes and other uses of public space.
  2. City contracting: Simplify contractor paperwork and adjust Procurement Policy Board requirements.
  3. Street safety and city property: Change review procedures for street projects, city property and office leasing.
  4. Building and waterfront permits: Centralize construction permitting and change waterfront permitting responsibilities.
  5. Rainy day reserves: Establish targets and a methodology for city budget reserves.

These are neutral summaries, not the complete legal ballot text. Read the official Yes/No explanations for each proposal.
